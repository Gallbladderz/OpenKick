**OpenKick** is a lightweight and autonomous client for the Kick.com streaming platform.

Currently, the app operates without authorization, providing the essential features you need to comfortably watch streams directly on your device.

**Features:**
* **Interface:** Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3, featuring Catppuccin themes.
* **Player:** Watch live streams, VODs, and clips powered by ExoPlayer.
* **Chat:** Real-time chat support during live broadcasts.
* **Background Playback:** Support for Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode and background audio.
* **Local Following:** Keep track of your favorite streamers and get local push notifications when they go live.

_Available in English, Spanish, and Russian._
